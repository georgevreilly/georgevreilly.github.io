Over the last two years,
I have traveled to multiple countries
with a single beefy USB charger,
even though those countries use a variety of `electrical plug types`__.

__ https://www.power-plugs-sockets.com/

How?
I bought a power cord for each kind of plug:

* USA + Canada: `Type A`__
* France, Portugal, Sweden: `Type C`__ (the Euro plug)
* Ireland + United Kingdom: `Type G`__
* New Zealand: `Type I`__

__ https://www.worldstandards.eu/electricity/plugs-and-sockets/ab/
__ https://www.worldstandards.eu/electricity/plugs-and-sockets/c/
__ https://www.worldstandards.eu/electricity/plugs-and-sockets/g/
__ https://www.worldstandards.eu/electricity/plugs-and-sockets/i/

Each of these power cords has the same `connector`__:
the `figure-8 C7`__,
since that's what the USB charger accepts.

__ https://www.sfcable.com/blog/all-know-power-cord-connectors
__ https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEC_60320#C7/C8_coupler

Similarly, I bring `clover-leaf C5`__ power cords with different plugs
for PC laptop chargers.

__ https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEC_60320#C5/C6_coupler

Obviously, I could use travel adapter plugs instead,
but I find it convenient to bring the right power cords.
And a selection of USB-C and USB-A cables.
